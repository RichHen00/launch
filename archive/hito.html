人 ひと Person, People

ひ hi と to

Usage: The kanji 人 represents "person" or "people".

Example: 入口 (いりぐち, iriguchi): Entrance (often seen on signs), 人 alone can also appear in contexts like "人注意" (ひとちゅうい, hito chūi), meaning "Caution: Pedestrians".
