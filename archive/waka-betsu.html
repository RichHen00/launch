別 わかべつ Separate, Different

わか waka べつ betsu

Usage: 別 commonly expresses “separate / different” in written Japanese.

Example: 別売 (べつうり, betsuuri): sold separately; 男女別 (だんじょべつ, danjo-betsu): separated by sex
